# Personal Portfolio Website — Design Brief

## Owner and purpose
Trevor Younger, freshman Sports Media major at Ithaca College. Create a personal portfolio for sports journalism and sports media opportunities.

## Audience and goal
Audience: classmates, professors, potential employers, and people working in sports journalism and broadcasting. Visitors should quickly understand my knowledge of sports, interests, accomplishments, and developing media skills.

## About me
I am from Perry Hall, Maryland, and study Sports Media at Ithaca College. I am interested in sports, broadcasting, and journalism.

## Projects and accomplishments
- Won a high school lacrosse championship two years in a row. Describe this accurately as a team accomplishment, not an individual award.
- Created detailed images and visual projects using online sourcing and creative tools. Provide room for examples and captions; do not invent specific project details.

## Sections
1. Home / hero with name, Sports Media student, short introduction, and optional headshot placeholder.
2. About Me.
3. Projects / Accomplishments, including lacrosse and creative visual work.
4. Interests / Skills, using only confirmed skills or clearly labeled placeholders.
5. Contact with LinkedIn link.

## Visual direction
Minimal, clean, professional, blue as the primary color. Mobile-friendly layout, readable typography, simple navigation, accessible contrast. Provide designated image placeholders for a headshot and sports photos.

## Contact
LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/trevor-younger-58a26a430/

## Content rules
Do not invent awards, work experience, jobs, statistics, technical skills, email addresses, photos, or project outcomes. Mark missing facts as TODO for confirmation. Keep language natural for a college student.

## Technical requirements
Use a simple static website suitable for GitHub Pages (HTML, CSS, and minimal JavaScript). Build one section at a time. Check at desktop and phone widths. Explain each meaningful change. Commit working milestones with Git when requested. Do not deploy without asking.

## Success criteria
A visitor can understand who I am, what I have done, and how to contact me in under a minute. The site works on a phone and can be published using GitHub Pages.
